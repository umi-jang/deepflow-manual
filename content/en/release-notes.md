## 2026 — 04 — 15 · v2.9

**Improvement** · **Fix**

### Forecast accuracy improvements and stability fixes

Average forecast accuracy improved by 6.2%, and several errors that occurred when processing large datasets have been fixed.

**Improvements**

- Enhanced seasonality pattern detection (concurrent monthly / weekly pattern learning)
- Adjusted outlier detection thresholds for more stable forecasts
- ~30% faster training speed on large SKU datasets

**Fixes**

- Fixed an issue where training jobs would stop under certain conditions
- Fixed display corruption for Korean SKU names

**Related:** [Forecast](/en/forecast) · [Dataset](/en/dataset)

## 2026 — 03 — 03 · v2.8

**New**

### Inventory simulation feature added

A new feature that lets you simulate inventory scenarios based on forecast results.

**New features**

- Compare scenarios by adjusting safety stock, order cycle, and lead time
- Real-time visualization of stockout risk and overstock ratio
- Export simulation results to Excel
