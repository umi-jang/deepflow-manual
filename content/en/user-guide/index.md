# DeepFlow Forecast User Guide

*AI-powered demand forecasting and inventory management that empowers your team*

DeepFlow is an AI solution that applies demand-forecasting technology to help you manage inventory, sales, and production predictively.

This manual walks through how to use DeepFlow Forecast and explains its key features so you can quickly understand and make the most of the service.

## Menu guides

- [Demand Forecasting](/en/forecast) — Six-month item-level demand forecasts, accuracy metrics, and AI-generated insight summaries.
- [Inventory Management](/en/inventory) — Predict shortages and surpluses, and automatically derive production and order quantities.
- [Business Intelligence (BI)](/en/bi) — Macro-level trends in shipments, sales, and inventory by category, with anomaly forecasting.
- [Market Intelligence (MI)](/en/mi) — Track historical volatility and short-term forecasts of external market indicators.
- [Dataset Management](/en/dataset) — Review dataset schemas, download templates, and upload your data.
- [S&OP](/en/sop) — Align sales and production plans across teams and finalize a single master plan.
- [User Management](/en/users) — Update your profile, change your password, invite users, and switch between apps.
- [Additional Considerations](/en/considerations) — How DeepFlow improves accuracy and recommended transition models.
