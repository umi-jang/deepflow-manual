## Demand forecasting overview

Demand forecasting is the core capability of DeepFlow. Because demand directly affects sales, production, and inventory, you need to predict it carefully and respond accordingly.

DeepFlow uses historical data and external variables to provide a six-month demand forecast for each item. For every item, you can see the demand pattern, expected volume, and the factors that influence demand.

## Reviewing predictions and accuracy in the grid

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/forecast/02.png" alt="Forecast grid" />
</Frame>

The grid surfaces key information for each item at a glance.

**Available information**

- **Accuracy**: Calculated from the difference between predicted and actual demand over the last 1–6 months.
- **Predicted sales volume**: Forecasted demand for the current month plus the next six months.
- **Change rate**: Variation between last month's actual demand and this month's predicted demand.

<Note>
  Accuracy is computed using **MAPE** (Mean Absolute Percentage Error). For example, a MAPE of 10% means the prediction differs from actual demand by 10% on average, and DeepFlow displays this as 90% accuracy (100 − MAPE).
</Note>

**Two ways to inspect the table**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse directly in the table">
    Click the item you want to inspect.
    <Note>
      You can drag and drop column headers to reorder them — your order is saved. Click a column name to toggle ascending or descending sort.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download as CSV">
    Use the download button to export the grid as a CSV file.
    <Note>
      The CSV mirrors exactly what is visible in the grid. If a filter is active and only some items are visible, only those rows are exported.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Analyzing past and forecast values in the chart

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/forecast/03.png" alt="Forecast chart" />
</Frame>

Compare historical sales with the next six months of forecasted demand.

- Hover over any point to view the historical demand (gray) and the predicted demand (blue).
- In the forecast region, an error band is overlaid based on the current cycle's prediction.

<Tip>
  Click **Show past predictions** to see the values that were predicted at earlier points in time. Because predictions are updated every cycle, comparing past predictions helps you gauge prediction volatility.
</Tip>

The **monthly chart** view compares the last three years of data side by side with the forecast, letting you check:

- Seasonality of demand
- Year-over-year demand shifts
- Consistency between actual results and predictions

### Past demand diagnosis

Based on the last three years of data for each item:

- Compare annual demand patterns to see the item's seasonality.
- Compare year-over-year demand to determine whether the trend is rising or falling.
- Compare those patterns against the most recent three months to confirm that the previous trend still holds.

### Forward-looking outlook

Using the next six months of predictions, you can examine the forecast pattern and the drivers behind it — including how strongly each factor correlates with the forecast.

<Note>
  This summary is generated from the **Factor analysis** tab. To inspect the drivers at a specific time point, open the Factor analysis tab and select that time point.
</Note>

### Risk and opportunity detection

Based on demand and inventory forecasts, DeepFlow suggests risks and opportunities to consider. You'll see angles relevant to sales, marketing, and SCM teams, along with concrete actions to take.

<Note>
  These recommendations do not encode your company's internal decision processes, so they may not be directly actionable. Treat them as a starting point to broaden your thinking rather than a final answer.
</Note>
