## Business Intelligence overview

Where the Demand Forecasting and Inventory Management modules work at the SKU level, Business Intelligence gives you a category-level view of the macro picture.

You can review historical shipments, sales, and inventory trends, and use demand forecasts to project where each metric is headed.

<Note>
  The **Sales** tab is only available if you have item-price data.
</Note>

## Shipment analysis and forecast

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/bi/01.png" alt="Shipment analysis screen" />
</Frame>

The **Current** tab shows shipment performance up through last month.

- Pick a year in the shipment trend analysis.
- Compare monthly shipment changes alongside year-over-year (YoY) and month-over-month (MoM) growth rates.
- Click any category to isolate it.

<Note>
  Only the top 5 categories by share are displayed.
</Note>

For more detail, the monthly share-by-category breakdown shows the items with the highest and lowest shipments each month.

The **Forecast** tab uses demand forecasts to provide:

- Forecasted monthly shipments by category
- Best and worst-performing items (Top 5 each) per month

This helps you pinpoint categories or time points with high shipment volatility, investigate the drivers, and plan a response.

## Sales analysis and forecast

The **Current** tab shows sales performance through last month. Sales equals shipment volume multiplied by unit price, so high-priced items contribute relatively more.

- Pick a year in the sales trend analysis.
- Compare monthly sales changes alongside YoY and MoM growth.
- Click any category to isolate it.

<Note>
  Only the top 5 categories by share are displayed.
</Note>

The monthly share-by-category sales breakdown reveals which items contribute the most and the least each month.

The **Forecast** tab provides:

- Forecasted monthly sales by category
- Best and worst-performing items (Top 5 each) per month

## Setting inventory management criteria

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/bi/02.png" alt="Inventory criteria configuration" />
</Frame>

Configure the thresholds DeepFlow uses to detect inventory anomalies.

**Key settings**

- **Stockout threshold (units)**: Items at or below this quantity are considered stocked out.
- **Shortage threshold (days)**: Items whose projected days-to-deplete fall below this number are flagged as low stock.
- **Overstock threshold (days)**: Items with projected days-to-deplete above this number are flagged as overstocked.
- **Watch categories**: Filter the view to just the categories you care about.

<Note>
  You can also define shortage and overstock sub-bands. Each must stay within its parent threshold. With these bands, you can prioritize the items that most urgently need a response.
</Note>

## Inventory analysis

The **Current** tab shows inventory through last month.

- Pick a year in the inventory trend analysis.
- Compare monthly inventory changes alongside YoY and MoM growth.
- Click any category to isolate it.
- Apply the configured thresholds to see the share of stockouts, shortages, and overstock for the current month.
- For this month's opening inventory, review the stockout item list and compare against each item's recent sales trend to determine whether the stockout is unexpected.

The **Inventory turnover analysis** lets you pick a month and review turnover by category and item. Using a trailing 3- or 6-month window, you can check operational efficiency.

<Note>
  Inventory turnover is computed as shipments divided by average inventory.
</Note>

## Forecasting inventory anomalies

Apply the configured thresholds to see the share of stockouts, shortages, and overstock for the current month.

Using forecast-derived days-to-deplete, DeepFlow surfaces the share of shortage and overstock, plus the item list. If you've defined sub-bands, you can see which items belong to each band and respond to the most urgent ones first.
