## Raw material price forecasting overview

Raw Material Price Forecasting helps you review the historical movement of raw material prices alongside likely future changes. DeepFlow combines multiple forecasting methodologies, market insights, and AI prediction models to deliver price forecasts and analytical content.

Forecasts are provided on both a monthly and weekly basis. The prediction models and underlying data are updated as the market evolves, and you can review not only the price forecast itself but also the factors that influenced the forecast, market news, market commentary, and short-term technical analysis.

## Price forecast main screen

On the main screen, you select a forecast period and an item to review the item's actual price, forecast price, accuracy, market volatility, and future direction.

### Forecast cycle and period

- **Monthly forecast**: Provided twice a month. Check the estimate on the 20th of each month, and the confirmed value on the third business day of the following month.
- **Weekly forecast**: Provided once a week, every Monday.

### Forecast item list

After you select a forecast period and item, you can review the item's main price indicators and forecast values.

- **Average price**: Cumulative actual average price for the month, based on previous trading days.
- **Current price**: Closing price as of the previous trading day.
- **Accuracy (100 − MAPE)**: Accuracy of the forecast compared to the actual price.
- **Market volatility**: Percentage indicator based on the difference between the high and low values for the month.
- **3-month / 6-month direction**: Difference between the 3-month or 6-month forecast and the month's average price.

<Note>
  MAPE is the Mean Absolute Percentage Error. It expresses the difference between actual and forecast values as a percentage of the actual value. DeepFlow reports `100 − MAPE` as the accuracy figure.
</Note>

### Forecast charts

- **Continuous forecast chart**: Compares actual and forecast values on a line chart, with market volatility and MAPE shown as bar charts.
- **Past forecast chart**: Displays the forecast value on each forecast date as a history view.
- **Lookback period**: Choose from 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, or 5 years.

The monthly outlook delivers a report that spans both past market analysis and future predictions. Reports are released on the 21st of each month and on the fourth business day of the following month, and combine DeepFlow's AI forecast models with the latest global market analysis.

The monthly outlook focuses on the following information.

- **Last month's market analysis and forecast performance review**: Revisit global raw material market movements and compare DeepFlow's forecasts with actual market prices to gauge forecast reliability.
- **Next month's raw material price forecast**: Review the future price trend and expected range produced by the AI forecast model.
- **Comprehensive news and market analysis**: Combines global economic indicators, supply chain issues, geopolitical risks, and the latest news data to explain the drivers behind price movements.

## Causal analysis

Causal analysis examines market dynamics to assess the likelihood of future price changes. It goes beyond simple correlation analysis to show how a specific factor influenced price movements and the time lag with which that influence appears.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/materials/03-causal-analysis.png" alt="Causal analysis screen" />
</Frame>

### Impact analysis by category

Factors related to the forecast item are grouped into **supply, demand, inventory, economy, finance, and other** categories, and each category's effect on price is expressed as an impact score. A higher value means the factor contributed more.

Click **View detailed analysis** on a category to see the analytical commentary and comparison charts that describe the relationship between the item-related factors and the forecast value.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/materials/04-category-impact.png" alt="Category impact analysis screen" />
</Frame>

### Detailed causal lag analysis

The detailed causal lag analysis examines the correlation between the forecast value and individual factors with a time lag applied. Use it to understand how long after a change in a factor its effect appears in the forecast value.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/materials/05-lag-analysis.png" alt="Detailed causal lag analysis screen" />
</Frame>

### Causal analysis chart

The causal analysis chart shows how much each factor contributed to price formation, expressed as a ratio.

- The upper chart decomposes the price level into a percentage contribution per category.
- In the lower chart, positive values represent upward drivers and negative values represent downward drivers.

### Monthly impact score comparison

Select a reference period and a comparison period to compare each category's upward or downward contribution to the forecast value. Looking at the most recent forecast month side by side with a past target month makes it easy to see how the drivers of price change have shifted.

## Market Intelligence

Market Intelligence brings together global news and market commentary related to the raw materials market. Use it alongside the forecast to understand the context behind price changes.

### Market news

Market news uses AI to analyze global news sources and provides easy-to-read summaries and translations that explain why the market is moving. DeepFlow monitors trusted sources across metals, energy and petrochemicals, agriculture, and macroeconomics around the clock and summarizes the key points.

## Analysis tools

Analysis tools offer an AI assistant and technical analysis features that help you interpret raw material prices faster.

The AI assistant is a conversational tool that analyzes complex market conditions and global economic indicators to support purchasing and materials decisions. Ask questions in natural language to get answers about prices, market commentary, risks, and opportunities.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/materials/11-ai-assistant.png" alt="AI assistant screen" />

Key capabilities include:

- **Real-time price and market analysis**: Review future price trends that reflect the latest market conditions.
- **Natural language Q&A**: Ask about items or market issues in plain language.
- **Tailored briefings**: Summarize the risk and opportunity factors for highly volatile items.

Use the AI assistant as follows.

1. Click the **AI assistant** icon at the bottom right of any DeepFlow screen.

3. Review the forecast scenarios and supporting evidence the AI provides, and use the recommendations as input to your purchasing decisions.

Example prompts:

- "Summarize next month's crude oil price outlook from an expert's perspective."
- "What are the main reasons behind the recent rise in rebar prices?"

### Technical analysis

Technical analysis is an analytical tool for projecting short-term price movements and trends. It assumes that demand, supply, and intrinsic value factors are already reflected in the price, and interprets the market by focusing on price action.

<Note>
  Technical analysis is based on daily data. Items that only have weekly data may not have technical analysis available.
</Note>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/materials/12-technical-analysis.png" alt="Technical analysis screen" />
</Frame>

Use technical analysis to monitor — on a daily basis, during the month or week — whether prices are moving in line with the AI forecast. When a market event makes a change in price direction likely, use it as a supplementary short-term price analysis tool.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/materials/13-technical-overview.png" alt="Technical analysis overview screen" />
</Frame>

Technical analysis covers the following items.

- **Overall indicator gauge**: Predicts the price direction one week ahead across five steps from strong upside to strong downside, with a probability for each scenario.
- **Price trend and forecast**: Provides a one-week-ahead price forecast in chart and table form, based on technical methodologies.
- **Technical analysis indicators**: Scores indicators related to direction, volatility, trend strength, and trend continuity to analyze price flow.

#### Direction

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/materials/14-direction.png" alt="Technical analysis direction indicator" />
</Frame>

- **Exponential Moving Average (EMA)**: A moving average that gives more weight to recent prices. The arrangement of EMAs over different periods is used to read the current market direction. When the 5-day EMA crosses above the 10-day EMA, treat it as an upward signal; when it crosses below, treat it as a downward signal.
- **MACD**: An indicator that uses the difference between a short-term and a long-term moving average. When MACD crosses above its signal line, it is an upward signal; when it crosses below, it is a downward signal. Also check whether MACD crosses above or below the zero baseline.

#### Volatility

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/materials/15-volatility.png" alt="Technical analysis volatility indicator" />
</Frame>

- **Bollinger Band**: Sets a standard-deviation range around a price moving average to analyze price volatility and trend. Widening bands indicate expanding volatility; narrowing bands indicate contracting volatility. Treat the upper band as resistance and the lower band as support.

#### Trend strength

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/materials/16-trend-strength.png" alt="Technical analysis trend strength indicator" />
</Frame>

- **RSI**: Expresses the strength of a price trend as a percentage and is used to estimate when a trend may reverse. An RSI of 70 or higher suggests an overbought condition and possible downside; 30 or lower suggests an oversold condition and possible upside.

#### Trend continuity

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/materials/17-trend-continuity.png" alt="Technical analysis trend continuity indicator" />
</Frame>

- **VIDYA**: A variable moving average that automatically adjusts its sensitivity based on market volatility. When the price sits above VIDYA, an uptrend is more likely; when it sits below, a downtrend is more likely. A steeper VIDYA slope indicates a stronger trend in that direction, while a flat or sideways VIDYA suggests the trend is weakening.
