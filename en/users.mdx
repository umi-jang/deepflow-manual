Change your personal settings and add other users to the service.

## [1] Viewing and editing your profile

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/users/01.png" alt="Profile screen" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review basic information">
    See the details you entered during email verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review recent sign-in info">
    Check when and where you last signed in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit profile">
    Click **Edit** to update your information. You can edit **name**, **department**, and **language**. Language can be Korean or English.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## [2] Changing your password

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/users/02.png" alt="Change password" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify with your current password">
    Enter your existing password to authenticate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the new password">
    Enter the new password and click **Save** to complete the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## [3] Managing users

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/users/03.png" alt="User list" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="View the user list">
    See all users currently registered to your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage existing users">
    Use the **More** menu on each user to manage them. Registered users can be removed here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite a new user">
    Click **Invite** to add a user.
    - Fill in the email, name, and department.
    - Pick the applications the invitee will be able to use (multiple selections allowed).
    - Click **Invite**. An invitation link is emailed to the user, and they can use the service after verifying their email.
  </Step>
</Steps>
