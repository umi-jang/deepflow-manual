      <h2 className="release-card-title">Item Management</h2>
      <p className="release-card-summary">A renewed Item Management menu lets you manage forecast-target items and set their status, attributes, owners, and forecast groups all in one place.</p>
      <div className="release-card-body">
        <img src="/images/release-notes/2026-07-16-item-management.png" alt="Item Management screen" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "10px", border: "1px solid #e5e7eb", marginBottom: "1.25rem" }} />
        <p>In the Item Management menu you can directly manage the items targeted for demand forecasting and maintain each item's metadata (status, attributes, owners). Items that sell in a series — such as product renewals or follow-up launches — can share past history for more accurate forecasting.</p>
        <p><strong>Items in the "Active" status are included in forecasting first, and the AI forecasts their future periods.</strong></p>
        <ul>
          <li><strong>Choose which items appear in the forecast menu:</strong> Items set to "Active" always appear in the forecast list. You can activate or deactivate the items currently under management yourself.</li>
          <li><strong>Manage custom attributes:</strong> For attributes you want to track separately — such as specification, region, or brand name — add them in [Manage attributes] and choose whether to show them in the list.</li>
          <li><strong>Manage release and discontinuation dates:</strong> Set a release date and discontinuation date per item. Accurate date management helps improve the forecast model's performance.</li>
          <li><strong>Manage discontinuation and new-product status:</strong> Set a discontinuation date, or set the release date and new-product threshold, to manage the product lifecycle status.</li>
          <li><strong>Assign owners per item:</strong> Assign an owner to each item. This will serve as the basis for future personalization features such as "show only my items."</li>
          <li><strong>Manage forecast groups:</strong> When you need to carry over a previous product's sales history — after a renewal or follow-up launch — group multiple items into a single forecast group managed as one forecast target. Past sales flow stays connected, improving forecast accuracy.</li>
        </ul>
